## Key Findings → PDBP Modules

| Finding | Evidence | PDBP module |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Citation skimming | 80% skim, rarely open | **Verification duties** (ranked actions) |
| Fact vs inference confusion | 60% struggle | **Decision Brief** sections split sourced/inferred |
| Excluded perspectives top ask | 60% Q8 | **Not addressed** section vs contract |
| Unstated assumptions | 40% Q8/Q10 | **Inferred** reasoning chains + contract |
| Unpredictable trust | 60% Q11 | **Calibrated Commit** feedback loop |
| Devil's Advocate appeal | 60% Q14 high-stakes | **Epistemic Fork** (same-source frames) |
| Repeated flagged errors | Vikas qualitative | Session claim memory (roadmap) |
| Code overconfidence | Srivatsan qualitative | **Mismatch alerts** on commit reveal |
| Journal privacy concern | Srivatsan Q16 | Opt-in only; no judgment profiling |

---

## Why ContextLens-Class Sidebars Failed Research Bar

Survey users asked for assumptions, gaps, and claim types, but **80% would not open optional panels** on a closure-inducing essay. PDBP responds by making the **brief itself** the primary UI, not a sidebar on prose that already signals "done."
