# Solution v3: The Human-Centric Decision Brief Protocol

**Author:** Srivenurajulu G  
**Date:** May 2026  
**Status:** Implementation Ready

---

Hey everyone! 👋 Welcome to version 3 of the Perplexity Decision Brief Protocol (PDBP). 

If you've been following along, you know that our main goal is to solve a very specific, yet massive problem: **Premature Epistemic Closure (PEC)**. That’s a fancy way of saying that users get a beautiful, polished AI essay, assume the research is totally done, and make big decisions without double-checking the facts. 

In our earlier versions, we created five awesome solutions to fix this. But they were scattered around. In v2, we tried to force them all into a strict 5-phase pipeline. But let's be honest, that felt a bit robotic.

**For v3, we’ve humanized the entire experience.** We want the AI to feel like a smart, collaborative research partner rather than a rigid compliance officer. 

Here is how the five core solutions come together in a natural, conversational, and deeply human way.

---

## 1. Intent Contract: Setting the Stage Before We Dive In

Imagine sitting down with a human analyst. Before they start Googling, they’d ask you: *"What exactly are we trying to decide here? And how high are the stakes?"*

That’s exactly what the **Intent Contract** does. Instead of a boring form, it acts as a quick alignment check right at the top of your search. If you ask a heavy question (like FDA regulations or interview prep), it casually asks you to confirm the stakes. Are they irreversible? Are they just operational? This frames everything that follows.

## 2. Epistemic Fork: Two Honest Perspectives

When the stakes are high (irreversible), there is rarely one single "right" answer. The AI shouldn't pretend there is. 

Enter the **Epistemic Fork**. If you’re researching a critical decision, the AI will honestly tell you: *"Hey, looking at the exact same sources, there are two ways to read this."* It gives you a Conservative reading and an Expansive reading. We then ask *you* to pick which one makes sense for your context and jot down a quick note on why. It forces you to pause and use your uniquely human judgment.

## 3. Calibrated Commit: A Gut-Check on Confidence

This is my favorite part. Before you take the brief and run off to make your decision, we do a quick gut-check called the **Calibrated Commit**. 

We show you a few key claims from the research—but stripped of their source labels. We ask: *"How confident are you in this statement?"* Once you rate it, we reveal the truth. Did it come from a strong source? Was it just an inference? This creates a powerful "Aha!" moment where you realize you might have been overly trusting. It's not a test to make you feel bad; it's a gentle nudge to calibrate your trust.

## 4. Research Debt Quantifier: Keeping Score of the Unknowns

We don't want to leave you guessing how much work is left. The **Research Debt Quantifier** is like a friendly progress ring that hangs out on your screen. 

Instead of a scary "Trust Score," this meter just tracks your legwork. Did you click the citations? Did you resolve the conflicting sources? Did you complete the Calibrated Commit? It’s a transparent way of saying, *"Here is how much investigation you still owe this decision before you act."*

## 5. Epistemic Fitness: Building Your Judgment Muscles

Finally, we want you to leave Perplexity sharper than when you arrived. **Epistemic Fitness** is a daily, 3-minute mini-game built right into the platform. 

It throws quick challenges at you—like spotting gaps in reasoning, identifying normative claims vs. factual ones, and evaluating source quality. Over time, this actually trains your brain to be a better consumer of AI-generated content. We even show your stats on a personalized Calibration Dashboard so you can see your own growth!

---

## Why v3 Wins

By weaving these five solutions together in a fluid, humanized way, we aren't just giving users better answers. We are building an environment that actively encourages **better decisions**. 

We’ve removed the friction of modal popups and separate pages. Now, it's just you and your AI research partner, working together to uncover the truth. Let's dive in! 🚀
